My Plan Works Perfectly for Me...

Hey guys, so I have a meal plan system that is totally awesome and works great for me. I am a busy single working woman who has to obviously manage my entire estate (tiny apartment) by myself. I developed this system over years of coming home exhausted, not wanting to cook, but not wanting to order takeout again, not just because of the amount of mystery ingredients, but also because the price for a flimsy burger is getting out of hand! I have my own energy, health, and time needs that I account for in my plan. Most of them are listed below. They may or may not work for you, but you can always adjust to your own needs.

I Have Limited Time and Energy

So my entire system is set up so that I can grab food and eat on my lowest energy days a nd still have a balanced and delicious meal. No more girl dinners that are actually half a family sized bag of hot cheetos, and a Cosmic Brownie

I Don't Work Weekends

Meaning I can do a lot of heavy lifting on Saturdays and Sundays. If that doesn't work for you, you just need to decide which days are best for you to spend 1-2 hours preparing for the rest of the week,

I Try to Focus on Quality Ingredients When Possible

Trust me, investing in high quality ingredients makes a world of difference in your cooking. I prioritize ingredient quality over price whenever possible. But that doesn't always mean the most expensive name brand item. I only do it where it counts.

I Also Love Takeout and Junk Food

I am not the nutrition police. Although I try to make healthy food choices as often as possible, I am also frequently at the Chick Fil A drive through and I love a slice of pizza to take the edge off. This is partially why I choose to cook a lot of my favorite fast foods at home, so that I can control exactly what goes into my food, even if it's not the healthiest in the world.

I am Not on a Diet

My meals are typically within a reasonable range: 500-800 calories. But I do sometimes go over this amount. I also am not prioritizing any sort of macro goal or anything like that. It's just food that I eat and enjoy while trying to maintain a balanced diet and lifestyle.

I Do Keep My Health In Mind

Am I constantly weighing my food and checking the protein count? No. Do I try to make food choices that will keep me fit and active well into my 90s? Yes! In fact, each month, I normally have a different goal in mind such as increased fiber, more dark greens, etc. They're not rules, more like small challenges that will benefit me in the long run.

I Cook a Lot of Things From Scratch

Look, I too am busy and exhausted 8 days out of the week. That's kind of the whole point of this meal plan. But It's worth it to me to make some items at home to save money, eat it the way I enjoy it, and have peace of mind about what is going into my food. If that's not your style, just by the precooked version, no big deal. But if you're down to throw on an apron maybe once per month, I promise it's not as hard as it looks.

My Main Pattern is "Big Prep, Little Prep"

Meaning I do a bigger prep for the first two weeks of the month, then smaller preps for each week to make sure I'm always eating fresh food.

I Don't Do Snacking

lol, yes I do! But I don't want cookies and chips to overrun my life. So I prep what I call "tiny meals" instead. I explain more of why I do this inside the meal plan.

Dairy is my One Food Sensitivity

I don't know if I'm Lactose Intolerant per se, but I don't do well with dairy except in very small doses (not even ice cream), so while I do use it in some meals I avoid it a mojority of the time. But that is the only food I'm fickle about. I eat red meat, pork, eggs, honestly I eat cheese in my sandwiches. I personally do not take any other allergens, sensitivities, or food modifications into consideration when cooking or meal planning.

It Might Work For You Too If...

  • You are busy with little time or energy to cook everyday.
  • You want to eat good quality food at home.
  • You're tired of wasting food and money every week.
  • You like variety, but don't like the stress of planning.
  • You keep saying you'll eat at home more, but don't have a system that sticks.
